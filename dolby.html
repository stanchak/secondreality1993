IN DOLBY SURROUND

The third title card says “in”, and then two glyphs: the Dolby double-D, which was already sitting in the demo’s own font. It reads as a joke. It was also true.

  “If you have a DOLBY SURROUND decoder/amplifier hooked up to your
   hi-fi set, have it on-line while running this demo and you will get
   some cool surround sound effects.
   (Surprisingly, does not work with the normal Sound Blaster).”
                                                  — README.1ST, 1993

The advertisement that shipped inside the ZIP put it in fewer words. This is FILE_ID.DIZ, the block a BBS pasted into its file listing:
