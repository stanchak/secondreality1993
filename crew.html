FUTURE CREW

Founded 1986 by Sami Tammilehto (Psi) and Jussi Markula (JPM) as a Commodore 64 group; on the PC by 1988. Never formally dissolved — the members went their own ways in the second half of the nineties. Their last release was Scream Tracker 3, July 1994.

Members went on to found or co-found Remedy (Max Payne, Alan Wake, Control), Futuremark (3DMark), Bugbear (FlatOut), Bitboys and Recoil Games — Remedy two years after this demo.
