HIDDEN SCREENS

Seven screens that are on no menu. Press /, type the word, press ENTER — or just pick one here.

Aliases work and so does any unique abbreviation, which is most of them: /ddstars, /386, /surround, /mail, /pxl, /stats, /logoff. TAB completes whatever you have started.

The demo has a hidden part of its own, which is what the first one is about. It is the only entry here that is a genuine 1993 secret rather than a 1993 detail worth a screen.
